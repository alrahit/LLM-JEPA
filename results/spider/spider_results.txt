--- CROSS-METRIC NOTE (unique to Spider) ---
The model with the BEST linearity (jepa/pred0, +20.8% R2) tied for the WORST accuracy.
The model with the BEST accuracy (pred2) had a weaker R2 gain than jepa. Linearity and
accuracy point in opposite directions at the extremes -- the strongest decoupling
finding across all three datasets tested (Synth, GSM8K, Spider).

--- KNOWN LIMITATIONS ---
- linalign's self-tuning controller shows the same tau=0.05 miscalibration bug found
  on GSM8K: lambda collapsed to ~1.6e-18 to 2.6e-18 (from init=1.0) by the end of
  training, driven by S values (~1e-7) far below the tau target. Confirmed across
  two datasets now -- systematic, not a one-off.
- Spider's overall accuracy (~2.8-3.0%) is low in absolute terms for all models,
  consistent with the known difficulty of cross-domain SQL generation for a 1.5B
  parameter model with a rank-8 LoRA adapter and limited training data.
